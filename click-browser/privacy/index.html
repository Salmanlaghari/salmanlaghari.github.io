Privacy Policy — Click Browser

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Click Browser ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by TEAM PK AI. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

  • Browsing data: History, bookmarks, and tabs are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload your browsing history to our servers.
  • Passwords: If you use the built-in password manager, credentials are encrypted and stored locally on your device.
  • AI chat: When you use Click AI, your prompts are sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate responses. Prompts are not stored by us.
  • Crash reports: Anonymous crash data may be collected to improve stability.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not show third-party ads (no advertising ID is used).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. V9 Shield (VPN)

The optional V9 Shield feature routes DNS queries through secure servers to protect your privacy. No browsing logs are kept.

4. Your Controls

  • Clear browsing data anytime from Settings.
  • Disable Click AI to stop prompts being sent.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data.

5. Children's Privacy

Click Browser is intended for users 18 and older.
